# Law 17 — Keep Others in Suspended Terror: Cultivate an Air of Unpredictability (30s, 5 scenes)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — The law
- Headline: "Be / unpredictable."
- Body: Predictable people are easy to read. Unpredictable ones keep everyone off balance.
- Visual: pill tag `48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 17`. A die draws itself and tumbles, its face flicking through values; question marks pop around it.

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — The mechanism
- Headline: "Patterns / get read."
- Body: If they can predict you, they can control you. Break the pattern and they lose their footing.
- Visual: two rows of shapes. Row 1 (PREDICTABLE) alternates circle/triangle; a dashed guess appears and matches (READ). Row 2 (UNPREDICTABLE) is erratic; the dashed guess is wrong and gets a red X (LOST).

## S3 (11.8–18.2s) — Observance: Fischer vs. Spassky, 1972
- Headline: "Chaos won / the crown."
- Body: In 1972 Bobby Fischer showed up late, made wild demands and forfeited a game. Spassky, rattled, lost the world title.
- Visual: a chess clock draws itself, hands sweeping; scoreboard starts FISCHER 0 – 2 SPASSKY, then counts up to 12½ – 8½ with Fischer in green.

## S4 (17.8–24.2s) — Apply it today
- Headline: "Keep them / guessing."
- Body: Now and then, do the unexpected. People who can't read you can't plan against you.
- Visual: a red targeting reticle (THEIR GUESS) chases a white dot (YOU) that jumps unpredictably; the reticle always lands where the dot used to be.
