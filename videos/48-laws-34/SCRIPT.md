# Law 34: Be Royal in Your Own Fashion: Act Like a King to Be Treated Like One

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Act like a king / to be treated like one." Carry yourself with quiet dignity, and people treat you as royalty.

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "Your bearing / sets your price." People take you at your own valuation. Ask for little and that is what you get.
Visual: self-drawing loop diagram: Act royal → They believe → They defer → You rise → (back), with "SELF-FULFILLING" at the center.

**S3 (12–18s) Transgression: Louis-Philippe.** Headline: "The king who / dressed down." The "Citizen King" traded royal pomp for a banker's umbrella; the French stopped respecting him and the 1848 revolution forced him to abdicate.

Visual: a caravel bobbing on drawn waves; three demand cards check off: Admiral of the Ocean Sea, Viceroy of new lands, a 0 → 10% count-up of all riches found.
