## S1 (0–6.2s): The law
- Headline: "Never appear too perfect."
- Body: Flawlessness breeds envy, and envy quietly works against you.

## S2 (5.8–12.2s): The mechanism
- Headline: "Envy wears a mask."
- Body: People rarely admit envy. It shows up as praise in public and sabotage in private.

- Headline: "Too just to survive."
