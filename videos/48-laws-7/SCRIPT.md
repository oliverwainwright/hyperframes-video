# Law 7 — Get Others to Do the Work for You, but Always Take the Credit (30s)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — The law
**Headline:** Let others work. Take the credit.
**Copy:** Use the skill, time and energy of others. Then make sure your name is on the result.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 7". Four worker figures draw themselves and lift a beam carrying a trophy; the trophy glows, its plate reads YOU, the workers fade grey ("THEIR SWEAT, YOUR NAME").

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — The mechanism
**Headline:** Credit sticks to who finishes.
**Copy:** People remember who delivered, not who did the work. Be the one who finishes and presents.
**Visual:** Relay pipeline IDEA → LABOR → POLISH → YOU PRESENT with a token travelling along it; two bars: WHO DID THE WORK (mostly grey, a sliver of accent) vs WHO GETS REMEMBERED (full accent).

## S3 (11.8–18.2s) — Transgression: Tesla and Edison
**Headline:** Tesla did the work. Edison kept the cash.
**Copy:** Nikola Tesla said Edison promised him $50,000 to fix his dynamos. When he delivered: "You don't understand our American humor."
**Visual:** A light bulb draws itself and lights up ("EDISON MACHINE WORKS · 1884"); count-up to $50,000 "PROMISED TO TESLA", then a red strike-through and "PAID: NOTHING".

## S4 (17.8–24.2s) — Observance: Rubens' studio
**Headline:** Rubens painted with many hands.
**Copy:** Peter Paul Rubens ran a studio of skilled assistants. They painted much of the work. He added the final touches, and his name.
**Visual:** An easel and canvas draw themselves; broad paint strokes fill in one by one (the assistants); a signature writes itself in the corner; badge "Sold as a Rubens" with a check.
