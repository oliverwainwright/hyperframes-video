# Law 22 — Use the Surrender Tactic: Transform Weakness into Power

## S1 — The law (0–6.2s)
**Headline:** Surrender to win.
**Body:** When you're weaker, don't fight for honor's sake. Bend like the reed, and you'll still be standing.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 22". An oak and a reed side by side; wind lines sweep across. The oak cracks (red) and topples; the reed bends with the wind and springs back. Labels "RESIST" (red) / "BEND" (green).

## S2 — Why it works (5.8–12.2s)
**Headline:** Yield now, win later.
**Body:** Resisting a stronger enemy invites destruction. Surrender buys you time to recover and strike back.
**Visual:** A self-drawing cycle: Yield → They relax → You buy time → You rise, around a centre label "WEAKNESS → POWER".

## S3 — Transgression: Melos (11.8–18.2s)
**Headline:** Melos refused to bend.
**Body:** When Athens demanded submission, the Melians refused. After the siege, the men were executed and the women and children enslaved.
**Visual:** An island; a ring of warships assembles around it as a count-up runs 0 → 38 "SHIPS SENT AGAINST MELOS" (Thucydides: 30 Athenian, 6 Chian, 2 Lesbian). The island turns red: "416 BC · MELOS DESTROYED".
