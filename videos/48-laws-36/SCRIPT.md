# Law 36: Disdain Things You Cannot Have: Ignoring Them Is the Best Revenge

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Can't have it? / Disdain it." Wanting what you can't get makes you look weak. Contempt makes you look untouchable.
Visual: a vine and grape cluster pop in; a reaching arrow stops short ("OUT OF REACH"); the reach withdraws, the grapes drain to grey and shrink: "SOUR GRAPES."

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "Attention is / what feeds it." Every reaction tells the world it matters; indifference starves it of oxygen.
Visual: split screen. "YOU REACT": four arrows of attention converge and the red circle swells ("IT GROWS"). "YOU IGNORE": the circle shrinks and fades ("IT FADES").

**S3 (12–18s) Transgression: Wilson vs. Pancho Villa.** Headline: "Wilson chased / Pancho Villa." After Villa's 1916 raid on Columbus, New Mexico, Pershing hunted him across Mexico. Villa was never caught and became a folk hero.
Visual: drawn border, red raid pulse, a pursuit trail snaking into Mexico with the army marker forever one step behind Villa; count-ups to 10,000 US troops and 11 months.

**S4 (18–24s) Observance: Henry VIII.** Headline: "Henry VIII / shrugged off Rome." The Pope refused his divorce. Henry stopped asking, treated Rome as irrelevant and broke away.
Visual: a sealed papal scroll ("NO DIVORCE") and a crown draw in; the scroll shrinks into irrelevance while the crown grows; badge "1534 · Head of his own Church" checks off.
