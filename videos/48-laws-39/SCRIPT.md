# Law 39: Stir Up Waters to Catch Fish

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Stir up the waters. / Catch the fish." Make your rivals angry and off balance. Then they are easy to catch.
Visual: a calm water line draws; a stick stirs it and ripples spread; fish dart in panic and one swims straight onto a green hook: "CAUGHT".

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "Angry people / make mistakes." Provoke a rival and they act before they think. Stay calm and you hold the advantage.
Visual: a CALM → RAGE gauge draws in three colored arcs; the needle swings into the red and trembles; an EMOTION bar fills while a JUDGMENT bar drains.

**S3 (12–18s) Example: Talleyrand and Napoleon (1809).** Headline: "Talleyrand let / Napoleon rage." Napoleon screamed at his minister, "sh*t in a silk stocking." Talleyrand never flinched; the court saw the emperor lose control.
Visual: Napoleon (bicorne hat) shakes amid red anger marks; Talleyrand stands still; count-up 0 → 30 "MINUTES OF RAGE · 1809".

**S4 (18–24s) How to apply it.** Headline: "Keep your cool. / Let them boil." When someone baits you, go still. Let them lose their temper and make the mistake.
Visual: two thermometers: THEM climbs 20° → 100° with boiling bubbles and a shake; YOU stays steady at 20°.
