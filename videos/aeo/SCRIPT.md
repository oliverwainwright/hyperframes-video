# What is AEO? — 30s script (5 scenes)

AEO = Answer Engine Optimization: structuring content so search engines, voice assistants and AI tools pick it as THE direct answer (featured snippets, voice answers, AI answers), often with no click. Sibling videos: SEO (rank in results), GEO (get cited in LLM answers).

Numbers: zero-click statistics conflict across 2025–2026 sources (56%/69%/65%+), so none are used. The only count-up is the illustrative 10 links -> 1 answer.

## Scene 1 (0–6.2s) Hook
- Headline: What is AEO?
- Body: Search used to give you ten links. Now it gives you one answer.
- Visual: tag "ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION", search bar types "how long to boil an egg?", 10 result rows stack in while a counter climbs 0 -> 10 LINKS, then the rows collapse into a single answer card and the counter drops to 1 ANSWER.

## Scene 2 (5.8–12.2s) Answer engines
- Headline: Meet the answer engines.
- Body: Featured snippets, voice assistants and AI chatbots don't list options. They reply with one answer.
- Visual: three icon cards (SNIPPET, VOICE, AI CHAT) draw themselves; three curves converge on a green "ONE ANSWER" pill.

## Scene 3 (11.8–18.2s) SEO vs AEO
- Headline: AEO = become the answer.
- Body: SEO earns you a ranking. AEO gets your content chosen as the answer.
- Visual: split panels. SEO: a ranking ladder where YOU climbs from 5 to 1. AEO: an answer card whose source chip "yoursite.com" lights up green. Below: trio chain SEO (ranked) -> AEO (the answer) -> GEO (cited by AI).

## Scene 4 (17.8–24.2s) How to get picked
- Headline: How to get picked.
- Body: Make the answer easy for a machine to find, lift and trust.
- Visual: a page wireframe (question heading, direct-answer box, schema chip); a 3-step checklist (Ask the real question / Answer it in two lines / Add schema markup) checks off, each step highlighting its part of the page.

## Scene 5 (23.8–30s) CTA
- Headline: Be the answer, not a link.
- Sub: Follow for more AI & tech explained in 30 seconds.
- Visual: headshot in circular frame (scale + fade), rotating dashed ring, drawn chat-bubble-with-check icon, tag "AEO, EXPLAINED IN 30s", 12 seeded particles drifting up.
