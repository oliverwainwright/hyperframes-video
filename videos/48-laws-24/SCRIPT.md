# Law 24 — Play the Perfect Courtier

**Headline:** Play the perfect courtier.
**Body:** Every circle of power is a court. Thrive there by walking the line between flattery and candor.

**Headline:** Power lives in the inner circle.
**Body:** Courtiers who read the room get closer to power. Those who blunder get pushed to the edge.
