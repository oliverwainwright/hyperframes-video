# Law 40: Despise the Free Lunch

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Nothing is free. / Despise the free lunch." Free gifts come with hidden strings. Pay full price and stay in control.
Visual: a gift box with a bow and a green "FREE" tag draws in; then a puppeteer's control bar appears above and red strings drop onto the box; the tag flips to red "OWED": "STRINGS ATTACHED".

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "A gift is a / hidden debt." Accept something free and you are in their debt. Paying your way keeps you free.
Visual: a balance scale draws; a small "FREE GIFT" lands on one pan, a heavy red "DEBT" block grows on the other and the beam tips toward "WHAT YOU OWE".

**S3 (12–18s) Transgression: Pizarro and Spain.** Headline: "Spain's easy gold / became a curse." In 1532 Pizarro seized the Inca emperor and a ransom of gold. Hooked on easy treasure, Spain chased El Dorado and slid into decline.
Visual: a room outline with a red line "AS HIGH AS HE COULD REACH"; gold bars stack up to it; year ticker 1500 → 1532; then the gold drains away: "THEN: CHASING EL DORADO".

**S4 (18–24s) Observance: the Medici.** Headline: "The Medici / paid their way." Florence's bankers spent generously on art, churches and allies. Their money bought loyalty, never debts.
Visual: a MEDICI hub with five spokes; gold coins flow out to ARTISTS, CHURCHES, ALLIES, SCHOLARS, THE CITY, each lighting green.
