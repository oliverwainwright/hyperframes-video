# Law 23 — Concentrate Your Forces

## S1 — The law (0–6.2s)
**Headline:** Concentrate your forces.
**Body:** Scattered energy just warms things up. Focused energy starts a fire.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 23". Parallel sun rays fall onto a lens, then converge to a single point on the ground, which ignites into a glowing burst. Labels "SPREAD OUT" → "FOCUSED".

## S2 — Why it works (5.8–12.2s)
**Headline:** One strong blow beats ten weak ones.
**Body:** Intensity defeats extensity. Spread yourself thin and nothing you hit lands hard enough to matter.
**Visual:** A wall. Top: five thin arrows ("SPREAD THIN") hit the wall and drop away in red. Bottom: one thick arrow ("CONCENTRATED") punches through; the wall cracks.

**Headline:** Five sons, one fortune.
**Body:** Mayer Amschel Rothschild sent his five sons to five European cities. They married within the family and kept the fortune in one house.
**Visual:** Schematic map: Frankfurt at the centre, lines drawing out to London, Paris, Vienna and Naples. Count-up 0 → 5 "SONS · CITIES", then a green ring closes around all five: "ONE FAMILY FORTUNE".
