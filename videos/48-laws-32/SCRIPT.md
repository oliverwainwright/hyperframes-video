# Law 32: Play to People's Fantasies (30s)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — Play to people's fantasies.
The truth is often dull. Offer the dream people crave and they will flock to you.
Visual: pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 32". A ground line splits the frame: grey rain falls over "REALITY" on the left; on the right an arched door draws itself and fills with a starry glow ("FANTASY"). Three small figures walk out of the rain into the door.

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — Reality disappoints. Fantasy sells.
Those who peddle hard truths get ignored. Those who sell wonder get power.
Visual: a vertical spine draws downward through four node circles: Daily life disappoints → People crave an escape → You offer the dream → They follow the dreamer (glowing, pulses).

## S3 (11.8–18.2s) — Venice wanted magic gold.
Alchemist Marco Bragadino dazzled Venice with visions of endless gold. The fantasy kept him rich, until the gold never came. (He was hanged in Munich in 1591.)
Visual: an alchemist's flask draws itself, bubbles rise, an arrow points to a gleaming gold coin that wobbles. Year ticker counts up to 1591 ("VENICE · GOLD PROMISED" → "HANGED IN MUNICH"); a red crack splits the coin and it dims.

## S4 (17.8–24.2s) — Sell the vision, not the specs.
Paint the life they want. Keep it close to real, but never too specific.
Visual: a grey card "WHAT IT IS: Features. Facts. Effort." gets struck through in red and dims; an arrow draws down to a glowing card "WHO THEY BECOME: Rich. Loved. Free." which pulses.
