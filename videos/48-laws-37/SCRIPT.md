# Law 37: Create Compelling Spectacles

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Don't tell them. / Dazzle them." People forget what you say. They never forget what you made them see.
Visual: a proscenium arch draws itself, an audience row appears, the curtains sweep open to reveal a radiant sun under two spotlight cones; the audience lights up.

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "The eye beats / the ear." Words invite argument. A spectacle skips the debate and lands straight in emotion.
Visual: two lanes. WORDS: a speech bubble draws, question marks pop, arrow to a red "DOUBT". IMAGES: an eye draws, pupil snaps in, arrow to a green "AWE".

**S3 (12–18s) Observance: Louis XIV, the Sun King.** Headline: "Louis XIV / became the sun." In 1662 he took the sun as his emblem and ran his court as theater. Versailles' Hall of Mirrors reflected him 357 times over.
Visual: a sun emblem with rotating rays; 17 mirrored arches rise and catch the light one by one; count-up 0 → 357 "MIRRORS · ONE KING".

**S4 (18–24s) How to apply it today.** Headline: "Use it today: / show, don't tell." Swap the paragraph for one unforgettable image, and stage it.
Visual: a "TELL" card of grey text lines gets a red X; a green "SHOW" card grows a bar chart with a self-drawing rising arrow.

**S5 (24–30s) CTA.** "Don't tell. Dazzle." + "Follow for more big ideas explained in 30 seconds." Headshot, sparkle icon, tag "LAW 37 OF 48, EXPLAINED".
