# Law 13 — When Asking for Help, Appeal to People's Self-Interest, Never to Their Mercy or Gratitude (30s, 5 scenes)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — The law
- Headline: "Appeal to / their interest."
- Body: Never ask for help through mercy or gratitude. Show people what they gain.
- Visual: pill tag `48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 13`. A figure marked THEM; two speech bubbles fly in, "PLEASE HELP ME" (gets a red X and fades) and "HERE'S YOUR GAIN" (green check, a connector draws to THEM).

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — The mechanism
- Headline: "Find the / overlap."
- Body: People act for themselves. Ask from where your need meets their interest.
- Visual: two self-drawing circles, YOUR NEED and THEIR INTEREST, slide together; the overlap fills green and is labelled YES.

## S3 (11.8–18.2s) — Greene's example: Corcyra and Corinth, 433 BC
- Headline: "Athens chose / its own gain."
- Body: Corinth reminded Athens of past favours. Corcyra offered its navy, the largest in Greece after Athens' own. Athens sided with Corcyra.
- Visual: a balance scale draws itself, pans labelled GRATITUDE (Corinth) and SELF-INTEREST (Corcyra); count-up 0 → 120 warships as the beam tips toward self-interest.

## S4 (17.8–24.2s) — Apply it today
- Headline: "Rewrite / the ask."
- Body: Lead every request with their gain, not your need.
- Visual: a message card. "Can you do me a favour?" is struck through in red; "Here's what's in it for you" types out beneath in green with a send arrow.
