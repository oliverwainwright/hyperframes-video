# Law 9 — Win Through Your Actions, Never Through Argument (30s)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — The law
**Headline:** Win with actions. Never argue.
**Copy:** Words breed resentment. A demonstration can't be argued with.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 9". Two red speech bubbles slam together and shake with a red spark ("ARGUING: NOBODY MOVES"), then dim; green blocks drop in and stack into a pyramid ("ACTING: PROOF").

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — The mechanism
**Headline:** Win the argument, lose the person.
**Copy:** Arguments make people dig in. When they see it with their own eyes, they convince themselves.
**Visual:** Two horizontal self-drawing pipelines: ARGUE: You push → You win → They resent (red); DEMONSTRATE: You show → They see → They agree (green).

**Headline:** Michelangelo changed nothing.
**Copy:** Piero Soderini said David's nose was too big. Michelangelo pretended to chisel and let marble dust fall. Soderini loved the "new" nose.
**Visual:** A statue on a pedestal draws itself, a red ring circles the nose, a scaffold appears, a sculptor taps and marble dust falls; the ring turns green. Count-up 0 → 17 FT, "DAVID · FLORENCE · 1504".

**Headline:** Argue with power, pay the price.
**Copy:** Consul Crassus ordered a large mast. An engineer sent a smaller one and argued it was better. Crassus had him flogged to death.
**Visual:** Two masts draw themselves (ORDERED tall, SENT short); a "BETTER!" bubble pops from the short one, a red X slams over it; typed "131 BC · FLOGGED".
