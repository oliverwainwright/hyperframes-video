# Law 41: Avoid Stepping into a Great Man's Shoes

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Don't step into / a great man's shoes." Follow a legend and you look like a smaller copy. Carve out your own path.
Visual: giant footprints march up "THEIR PATH"; small footprints step into them and dim; a green path forks away with new green footprints: "YOURS".

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "The heir starts / in the shadow." Their legend is already built. Just to stand out, you must achieve twice as much.
Visual: bar chart. "THE LEGEND" bar rises and sets a reference line; "THE HEIR" bar reaches only that line and gets labeled "JUST A COPY"; a green target line appears and a count-up 1.0× → 2.0× "TO OUTSHINE" as the heir's bar climbs to double and turns green.

**S3 (12–18s) Transgression: Louis XV.** Headline: "Louis XV lived in / the Sun King's shadow." In 1715 a five-year-old inherited the crown of a legend. Louis XV never escaped the comparison, and the monarchy decayed.
Visual: a giant crowned Louis XIV and a small Louis XV ("AGE 5"); a shadow sweeps over the small figure; count-up 0 → 72 "YEARS ON THE THRONE BEFORE HIM".

**S4 (18–24s) Observance: Alexander the Great.** Headline: "Alexander outran / his father." Afraid Philip would leave him nothing great to do, he went his own way and conquered all the way to India.
Visual: "PHILIP'S KINGDOM" circle; a route draws Macedon → Egypt → Persia → India with stops popping in, ending in a green arrowhead; badge: Alexander "the Great" checks off.
