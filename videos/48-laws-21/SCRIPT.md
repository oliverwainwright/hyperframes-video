# Law 21 — Play a Sucker to Catch a Sucker: Seem Dumber Than Your Mark

## S1 — The law (0–6.2s)
**Headline:** Seem dumber than your mark.
**Body:** Nobody likes feeling less intelligent. Let them feel smart, and they'll never see you coming.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 21". A face with a goofy mask (big round eyes, wobbly mouth, bobbing "?"). The mask lifts away to reveal narrow, sharp eyes and a smirk; outline turns indigo. Labels "WHAT THEY SEE" → "WHAT'S REAL".

## S2 — Why it works (5.8–12.2s)
**Headline:** Feeling smart drops their guard.
**Body:** When people feel smarter than you, they stop suspecting you. Their ego does your work.
**Visual:** Two stat bars with count-ups: THEIR EGO fills to 95%, THEIR GUARD drains to 10%. Result chip "EASY TO READ".

## S3 — Observance: the Great Diamond Hoax (11.8–18.2s)
**Headline:** The hicks who fooled the bankers.
**Body:** In 1872, prospectors Philip Arnold and John Slack played naive country boys. San Francisco's top financiers bought their "diamond field", salted with gems they had bought themselves.
**Visual:** Hills draw themselves; diamonds drop into the ground (salting); top hats of the financiers appear and lean in. Year ticker rolls to 1872, caption "THE GREAT DIAMOND HOAX".

## S4 — How to apply it (17.8–24.2s)
**Headline:** Let them play the genius.
**Body:** Ask the naive question. Let them explain. The less threatening you seem, the more you learn.
**Visual:** Two speech bubbles: YOU ("How does that work?") and THEM (text lines growing as they explain). Info dots stream from their bubble to yours. Caption "THEY RELAX · YOU LEARN".
