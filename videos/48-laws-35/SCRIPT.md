# Law 35: Master the Art of Timing

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Wait for the moment. / Then strike." Power belongs to those who know when to wait and when to move.
Visual: a self-drawing hourglass drains; below, a timeline marker slides past "TOO EARLY" to a green "NOW" ring, a lightning bolt strikes; "LATE" sits beyond.

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "Read the tide, / not the clock." Power moves in waves. Patience lets you see the wave coming; speed lets you ride it.
Visual: a sine "tide" draws across; a rider dot travels through the "WAIT" trough to the crest marked "STRIKE" (green ring); "TOO LATE" on the far trough.

**S3 (12–18s) Observance: Joseph Fouché.** Headline: "Fouché always / switched in time." He read each shift early: revolutionary, Robespierre's undoer, Napoleon's police chief, then the King's minister. He outlasted them all.
Visual: staircase of regime chips (1793 Revolution, 1794 Terror ends, 1799 Napoleon, 1815 The King) lights up step by step while a year ticker counts 1789 → 1815 and turns green: "SURVIVED EVERY REGIME".

**S4 (18–24s) How to apply it.** Headline: "Master three / kinds of time." Greene's long time (patience), forced time (rush your rivals), end time (strike fast).
Visual: three cards with drawn icons: a clock with a slow sweeping hand, a clock squeezed by red arrows, a green lightning bolt.
