# Law 3 — Conceal Your Intentions (30s)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — The law
**Headline:** Conceal your intentions.
**Copy:** Keep people off balance and in the dark. Never reveal the real goal.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 3". A red target draws itself ("WHAT YOU AIM AT: HIDDEN"), then a smiling mask slides up and covers it ("WHAT THEY SEE: A SMILE").

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — The mechanism
**Headline:** Show your hand, lose the game.
**Copy:** Once people know your aim, they can defend against it. Keep them guessing and they can't prepare.
**Visual:** Branching flowchart from "Your real goal": REVEALED → They prepare → They resist → Blocked (red) vs CONCEALED → They relax → You move first → Achieved (green).

## S3 (11.8–18.2s) — Observance: Bismarck
**Headline:** Bismarck praised his next enemy.
**Copy:** In 1850 Otto von Bismarck argued for peace with Austria. It won him a top diplomatic post. In 1866 he went to war with Austria.
**Visual:** PRUSSIA and AUSTRIA circles joined by a green peace line with a check; year ticker 1850 → 1866; the line turns red and a red X replaces the check ("WAR WITH AUSTRIA").

## S4 (17.8–24.2s) — How to apply it: the red herring
**Headline:** Use a red herring.
**Copy:** Show a believable decoy. While everyone watches it, move toward the real goal.
**Visual:** From YOU, a bright path draws to a decoy flag ("WHAT THEY WATCH"); a dim path draws to a star; a green dot travels the quiet path and the star lights up green.

## S5 (23.8–30s) — CTA
**Headline:** Keep your cards hidden.
**Sub:** Follow for more big ideas explained in 30 seconds.
