# What is GEO? — 30s script (5 scenes)

Sources: Aggarwal et al., "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" (Princeton / KDD 2024) — adding citations, quotations and statistics lifted source visibility in generative-engine answers by up to 40%.

## Scene 1 (0–6.2s) — Hook
- Headline: **What is GEO?**
- Explanation: Generative Engine Optimization. Getting your brand cited inside AI-written answers.
- Visual: big "GEO" letters; each letter unfolds into its word (Generative / Engine / Optimization). A chat input box types a question ("best CRM for a small team?").

## Scene 2 (5.8–12.2s) — AI writes the answer
- Headline: **No list. One answer.**
- Explanation: ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews write one answer and cite only a few sources.
- Visual: AI chat bubble whose text lines draw in, then citation chips [1] [2] [3] pop in; one chip turns green, labelled "YOU". Engine pills underneath.

## Scene 3 (11.8–18.2s) — SEO vs AEO vs GEO
- Headline: **Get quoted, not just ranked.**
- Explanation: SEO ranks you. AEO makes you the answer. GEO gets you quoted inside it.
- Visual: three stacked cards draw in with mini icons: ranked list (SEO), answer box (AEO), chat bubble with a citation (GEO, highlighted).

## Scene 4 (17.8–24.2s) — What AI likes to quote
- Headline: **Give AI something to quote.**
- Explanation: Stats, expert quotes and cited sources lifted AI visibility by up to 40% in Princeton's GEO study.
- Visual: checklist with self-drawing checkmarks (Hard numbers / Expert quotes / Cited sources); count-up 0 -> 40% with a growing bar.

## Scene 5 (23.8–30s) — CTA
- Headline: **Be the source AI quotes.**
- Sub: Follow for more AI & tech explained in 30 seconds.
- Visual: headshot in circular frame (scale + fade), dashed rotating ring, seeded upward particles, tag "GEO, EXPLAINED IN 30s".
