# Law 31: Control the Options: Get Others to Play with the Cards You Deal (30s)

## S1 (0–6.2s) — Control the options.
Let them choose, but make every option one that favors you.

## S2 (5.8–12.2s) — The illusion of free choice.
People fight orders, but they obey their own choices. So narrow the menu.
Visual: a funnel draws itself; six colored option dots ("THEY FEEL FREE") pop in across the top and slide down into the funnel, emerging as one green dot: "YOU PICKED THE RESULT".

## S3 (11.8–18.2s) — Ivan walked off his throne.
Ivan the Terrible left Russia two options. Both meant Ivan wins. The people begged him to return.

Kissinger fed Nixon options framed so his favorite looked best. Shape the choices and the decision makes itself.
