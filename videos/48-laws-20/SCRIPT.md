# Law 20 — Do Not Commit to Anyone

## S1 — The law (0–6.2s)
**Headline:** Don't commit to anyone.
**Body:** Let everyone want you, and let no one own you.
**Visual:** Pill tag "48 LAWS OF POWER · LAW 20". YOU at the centre inside a rotating dashed ring; three suitors (A, B, C) reach toward you with self-drawing lines that stop short of the ring. Label "OWNED BY NONE".

## S2 — Why it works (5.8–12.2s)
**Headline:** Commitment kills leverage.
**Body:** Undecided, you hold the balance. Commit, and one side owns you while the other turns against you.
**Visual:** A balance beam draws itself on a fulcrum; the YOU ball sits in the middle ("BALANCED · BOTH SIDES COURT YOU"). The ball rolls to Side A, the beam tips, Side B turns red ("COMMITTED · ONE SIDE OWNS YOU").

## S3 — Observance: Elizabeth I (11.8–18.2s)
**Headline:** The queen who never said yes.
**Body:** Elizabeth I kept foreign princes hoping for her hand for decades, and never married. Every suitor stayed eager to please.
**Visual:** A crown draws itself; six suitor rings drift in from the edges, stop short and fade. Count-up 0 → 44 "YEARS ON THE THRONE", then "NEVER MARRIED" in green. Ghost "1558".

## S4 — How to apply it (17.8–24.2s)
**Headline:** Keep them competing.
**Body:** Let people compete for you. Your freedom is your power.
**Visual:** Self-drawing vertical timeline with three steps: Hear every offer / Promise nothing / Let them compete, ending in a green check.
