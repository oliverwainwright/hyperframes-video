# Law 38: Think as You Like but Behave Like Others

**S1 (0–6s) The law.** Headline: "Think as you like. / Act like the crowd." Keep radical ideas private. In public, blend in.
Visual: a row of seven identical grey figures draws in; only the middle one lights up inside: a glowing mind, thought bubbles and a cloud with a green star. "SAME OUTSIDE. YOUR OWN INSIDE."

**S2 (6–12s) Mechanism.** Headline: "Flaunt it and / you pay for it." Showing off different views reads as contempt; people make you pay for making them feel inferior.
Visual: a branching flowchart from "Unusual ideas": left branch Flaunt them → Others feel judged → They punish you (red); right branch Keep them private → You blend in → Free to think (green).

**S3 (12–18s) Transgression: Pausanias.** Headline: "A Spartan hero / went Persian." He led the Greeks to victory at Plataea (479 BC), then flaunted Persian dress and manners. Sparta grew suspicious and walled him inside a temple to starve.
Visual: a Spartan helmet, an arrow, Persian tiara and robe; ticker 0 → 479 BC "VICTOR AT PLATAEA"; then red bricks stack over the Persian figure: "WALLED IN BY HIS OWN CITY".

**S4 (18–24s) Observance: Tommaso Campanella.** Headline: "Campanella / wore the mask." Jailed by the Inquisition, he played along. His book "Atheism Conquered" claimed to refute heresy while laying out every heretical idea.
Visual: a conformist mask draws and tilts; a book titled "ATHEISM CONQUERED" draws in and ideas (sparks) fly out of it: "IDEAS SPREAD".
