# Law 45: Preach the Need for Change, but Never Reform Too Much at Once (30s)

- Headline: "Preach change, reform slowly."
- Body: People crave improvement but fear upheaval. Too much change at once triggers revolt.

## S2 (5.8–12.2s): The mechanism
- Headline: "Habits feel safe."
- Body: Swap one piece at a time and the change feels familiar. Swap everything and people fight back.
- Visual: two rows of six blocks. GRADUAL: blocks turn indigo one at a time → "FAMILIAR → ACCEPTED". ALL AT ONCE: every block flips red at once and shakes → "SHOCK → BACKLASH".

- Headline: "Too much, too fast."

- Body: Frame each change as an improvement on the past, not a break from it.
